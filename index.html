Questions people ask

If yours isn't here, email and you'll get a straight answer the same day.

Is it really made with AI?

Yes, and we'd rather you knew. The product is yours: we work from your photos and your real packaging. The person on screen is a real person who has agreed to appear in ads. What the AI replaces is the crew, the studio and the month.

Will it look fake?

Look at the reel and decide for yourself. A wrong label, a strange hand or a face that's off gets remade, not hidden. If we can't make your product look right, we'll say so before you've spent anything.

Do I have to send you the product?

No. Good photos are enough. If it's something unusual we might ask you to post one over.

How long does it take?

A spec ad usually comes back within a few days. A test pack takes about a week, most of which is waiting for you to sign off the scripts.

What if I don't like it?

Then you don't pay for it. The spec ad costs nothing until you decide to use it, and every paid order includes changes.

Can I run the ads on Meta, TikTok and YouTube?

Yes. Every paid order includes the right to run the ads as paid ads for 90 days on any platform.

What if I want to run one for longer?

Keep running an ad past 90 days for 30 percent more. Just email and say which one.

Who am I dealing with?

Bohan Sun. He's in London, he reads every email, and he makes the ads. There's no account manager and no sales team.
