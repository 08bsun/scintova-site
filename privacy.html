Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Who we are

Scintova is an AI video ad studio in London, run by Bohan Sun. When this page says "we", it means Scintova. You can reach us at hello@scintova.com.

What this website collects

Nothing about you on purpose. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers, and there's nothing to sign in to. Our fonts are stored on this site, so your browser doesn't contact any font service either.

The brief form on the home page doesn't send anything to us. It opens your own email app with your answers filled in, and nothing leaves your device until you press send.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. GitHub's privacy statement explains how it handles that.

When you email us

We receive your name, your email address and whatever you send, such as links to your products, product photos and anything you tell us about your brand. We use it to:

  • reply to you and write your scripts,
  • make and deliver your ads,
  • send invoices and keep our business records.

We rely on your request and our agreement with you to do this, and on our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your information or share it for marketing. We only use it with the services that run the studio: our email provider, Microsoft 365, and the computing services we use to make the ads.

How long we keep it

For as long as we're working together, and after that for as long as we need it for our records, including tax records. If we never end up working together, we delete your email and files when they're no longer useful.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email hello@scintova.com and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
